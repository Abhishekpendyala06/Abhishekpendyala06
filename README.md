## 🧭 About Me

I'm a builder-first AI/ML engineer who turns messy real-world problems into working, production-grade software — not notebooks that only run on my laptop. Over the past year I've shipped a **multimodal RAG-based HR assistant** used across 11 job roles and a **multilingual agricultural chatbot** designed for 140M+ Indian farmers, both under an internship at Citta AI. I care about systems that actually reach users: clean retrieval pipelines, grounded LLM outputs, and frontends people enjoy using.

Currently deepening my focus on **Agentic AI, RAG architectures, and fine-tuning** — while wrapping up my final year at CBIT.
