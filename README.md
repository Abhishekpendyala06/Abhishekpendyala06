## 🧭 About Me

I'm an AI/ML engineer who thinks in systems, not scripts. My interest isn't in models that answer questions — it's in **agents that reason, retrieve, decide, and act**, and in the infrastructure that makes that reliable at scale: grounded retrieval, role-aware context, tool-calling, and orchestration across multiple agents working toward one goal.

I approach engineering the way a systems architect would: understand the failure modes first, design for the edge cases, then build. That mindset has carried through real internships where I owned production-grade AI applications end-to-end — from data ingestion and retrieval architecture to the frontend a real user actually touches. I don't treat AI and full-stack engineering as separate disciplines; a model that never ships past a notebook hasn't solved anything yet.

I'm drawn to the parts of this field that are still being defined — agentic architectures, the Model Context Protocol, multi-agent coordination, retrieval systems that ground truth instead of approximating it. I'd rather spend a weekend reverse-engineering a new agent framework than wait for a course to teach it to me. That curiosity, paired with a CGPA of 8.80 in my final year at CBIT, is what I bring to every team I work with: someone who learns fast, ships faster, and doesn't need hand-holding to go from concept to working system.
