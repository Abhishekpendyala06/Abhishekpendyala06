# 🚀 Engineering Intelligent Systems That Think, Reason & Act

I'm an AI/ML engineer passionate about designing intelligent systems that **reason, retrieve, plan, and execute** rather than simply generate responses. My work centers around **Agentic AI**, **Large Language Models**, **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**, **tool-calling**, and **autonomous multi-agent systems** capable of solving real-world problems.

I enjoy building complete AI products—from backend intelligence and retrieval pipelines to intuitive frontend experiences. To me, an AI system isn't complete until it is scalable, production-ready, and delivers measurable value to users.

My engineering philosophy is simple:

> **Understand the problem deeply. Design reliable systems. Build elegant solutions.**

Whether developing enterprise AI copilots, multilingual assistants, or autonomous AI workflows, I strive to combine software engineering best practices with cutting-edge AI research to build systems that are robust, explainable, and practical.
